# A3 — Clinical Consequence Mapping

## Objective

A3 maps experimentally observed A2 sequencing-error classes to their possible
functional, variant-interpretation and clinical consequences.

## Initial loci

- L1: original Experiment E target on chromosome 20
- L2: chr1:20061156 A>T
- L3: chr4:40028853 A>G

## Required distinction

A3 will separate:

1. experimentally demonstrated A2 effects;
2. database-derived functional annotation;
3. inferred clinical interpretation risks;
4. clinically validated outcomes.

## A2 error classes to map

- ALT evidence loss;
- ALT to REF transition;
- ALT to deletion transition;
- ALT to other transition;
- reduced ALT-supporting read count;
- reduced variant QUAL;
- preserved heterozygous genotype despite evidence suppression.

## Planned outputs

- candidate-locus annotation table;
- gene and transcript consequence table;
- ClinVar and population-frequency summary;
- error-to-clinical-impact matrix;
- A3 findings document;
- decision on whether an additional clinically relevant Dorado locus is needed.
